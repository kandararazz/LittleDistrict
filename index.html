About LittleDistrict

Dubai Kids & Family Neighborhood Network

LittleDistrict is an intelligent, friendly community web platform built specifically for Dubai parents and families to easily connect, organize neighborhood activities, pass along or sell school supplies, and discover top local kid spots.
